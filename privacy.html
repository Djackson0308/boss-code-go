07 Third Party Services +

GO may display or deliver content using third party services, including video, maps, payments, media delivery and other technologies.

Technical information may be transmitted to a provider when necessary to deliver a requested feature.

Cloudflare provides hosting and network information, Stripe handles payment entry and processing, Resend supports sign-in and marketing email, and YouTube or Google provides embedded video. Embedded players load directly from their provider, which may receive network, device and playback information. GO uses both standard YouTube embeds and privacy-enhanced embeds. Provider practices may vary by player and user settings. Read the providers' privacy policies for details.

08 Security +

B.O.S.S. CODE MEDIA GROUP LLC uses reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse or destruction.

Measures may include encrypted connections, authentication controls, access restrictions, server side authorization, database protections and account separation.

No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

09 Data Retention +

B.O.S.S. CODE GO retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service and provide the features requested by the user.

Challenge Information

Challenge progress, written responses, reflections, achievements, streaks and related history may remain connected to the user's account for as long as the account remains active.

Keeping this information allows users to review their progress, completed challenges and personal growth history over time.

If GO provides an option to delete individual information, users may remove that information sooner.

Other Information

Certain business, transaction, security or legal records may be retained for longer periods when reasonably necessary for accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or compliance with applicable law.

10 Account and Data Deletion +

Users may request permanent deletion of their B.O.S.S. CODE GO account and associated personal information.

Account deletion requests may be submitted by contacting B.O.S.S. CODE MEDIA GROUP LLC at greatness@bosscodemedia.com.

To request deletion in the app, open Decision Makers, go to My Profile and choose Delete Account. Follow the confirmation prompts.

When a permanent account deletion request is completed, associated personal data will be deleted or anonymized unless limited retention is necessary for legitimate legal, security, fraud prevention, accounting or regulatory purposes.

Associated data may include:

  • Profile information
  • Profile images
  • Challenge progress
  • Written challenge responses
  • Private reflections
  • Tracker information
  • Achievements
  • Course progress
  • Community account information
  • Other personal information associated with the account

Removing the B.O.S.S. CODE GO application from a device does not automatically delete the user's account.

Account deletion may not automatically cancel a subscription managed through Apple, Google or another payment provider.

13 Location and Maps +

GO may display restaurant locations, businesses, venues and other geographic information.

GO stores approximate country, region and city with usage events, based on Cloudflare network information. This analytics flow uses network information to estimate location.

If a future feature requires device location, the appropriate device permission and disclosure will be provided.

14 Notifications and Marketing Email +

GO may offer notifications for app activity, updates, reminders, content or other app features.

Users may manage notification permissions through their device settings. GO promotional emails require a recorded affirmative opt-in. Creating an account, making a purchase, or having an existing CRM contact record does not grant this consent. We record the choice, consent notice version and time. You can change your choice on the email preferences page, use the unsubscribe link in a marketing email, or contact GO support. Withdrawing consent stops promotional delivery, including messages still queued for sending. Sign-in codes and order communications serve separate account and transaction needs.
