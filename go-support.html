B.O.S.S. CODE GO Support

For help with the app or a Code Clothing order, email B.O.S.S. CODE Media.

greatness@bosscodemedia.com

Describe what happened and include your device model, iOS version, and app version if known. For an order question, include your order number. Keep passwords, sign-in codes, and payment card details out of your message.

Connection and playback

Check your internet connection, reopen the app, and try the refresh button. If a video won’t play in the app, tap Open on YouTube when available. Include the title when reporting a playback issue.

Sign-in

Confirm the email address you entered and check your spam or junk folder for the sign-in code. If you requested multiple codes, use the latest one.

Code Clothing orders

If checkout closes before the app shows a confirmation, check your email for an order receipt before retrying. Contact support if you’re unsure whether the order went through.

Privacy and account deletion

Read the B.O.S.S. CODE GO privacy policy.

To delete your account in the app, open Decision Makers, go to My Profile, and choose Delete Account. Follow the confirmation prompts. For help accessing your account or completing deletion, email support.
